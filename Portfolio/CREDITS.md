## Probe-Fed Patch Array

Design and analysis: Leo Alvelais. The [design report](antenna-designs/4ghz-probe-fed-patch-array/docs/circular-taylor-array-design-report.pdf)
covers the earlier 2.4 GHz revision. The current project is listed at 4 GHz, with older setup frequencies identified
in the revision notes. The MATLAB aperture studies use wavelength-normalized coordinates.

The additional Python aperture plots reproduce the supplied MATLAB calculation. They are analytical array-factor
results, separate from the HFSS unit-cell figures and finite-array model.

## UMS Low Noise Amplifier

Report author: Leo Alvelais. The initial circuit workflow follows Anurag Bhargava's UMS LNA tutorial. The layout,
back annotation, and RFPro results are described in the [report](MMIC-designs/2500mhz-ums-lna/docs/ums-lna-report.pdf).
